Paint enhancement & protection

More than clean.
Protect the finish.

Great results begin with the right preparation. We inspect and clean the paint first, correct defects when appropriate, and then finish with the protection that fits your goals—from wax or sealant to a professional ceramic coating.

Long-term protection

Ceramic coatings

Deeper gloss. Easier upkeep.

A ceramic coating bonds to properly prepared paint to create a durable, hydrophobic layer. It helps the surface resist environmental contamination, makes routine washing easier, and delivers the rich gloss customers love.

  • Enhanced gloss and slickness
  • Water-beading, hydrophobic performance
  • Easier maintenance and cleaning
  • Added resistance to environmental contaminants
  • Professional preparation before installation
Ask about ceramic coating ↗
Restore the finish

Paint correction

Bring clarity back to your paint.

Paint correction uses controlled machine polishing to reduce visible swirl marks, light scratches, haze, and oxidation. The goal is a cleaner, clearer reflection and a stronger foundation for the protection applied afterward.

  • Paint inspection and careful preparation
  • Reduction of swirls, haze, and light defects
  • Improved depth, gloss, and reflection
  • Wax or sealant available for finishing protection
  • Ideal preparation before ceramic coating
